Edges removed — 2026-09-10

Every entry above this line describes a system with a connection graph, an
electrician holding it, and wired units. That system no longer exists. The
entries are left as written: they are the record of runs that actually
happened, and rewriting them would destroy the evidence rather than update it.
See HISTORY.md section 17.

What replaced it:
  nw-spawn   forks one supervisor per unit, double-forked so PID 1 adopts the
             houses, reports the pids, and EXITS. Boot-time only.
  PID 1      requires a complete pid report AND WIFEXITED with status 0.
             Successful exit is the completion signal; there is no death to
             watch for and no HALT-on-broker-death path.
